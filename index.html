The short answer

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product discovery list used to organize shopping links and comparison notes. A useful list may include categories, seller links, listed prices, measurements and QC references. A listing alone does not verify quality, authenticity or availability.

This page provides an Oopbuy shopping guide and direct access to CAFANS categories. It is not a downloadable spreadsheet, a live inventory feed or an official Oopbuy service.

From discovery to decision

Make Oopbuy finds
easier to compare.

A useful shortlist records the same details for every item. Use these four steps to turn scattered product links into a practical comparison.

01

Choose the category

Separate shoes, clothing and accessories first. Save the seller link, product title and selected variant alongside your Oopbuy product links.

02

Check the fit

Compare the item’s measurements with something you already own. Record units and distinguish body measurements from garment measurements.

03

Review the evidence

Use available Oopbuy QC photos and seller information to inspect visible details. Match the pictures to the exact color, size and version.

04

Estimate the total

Add delivery and service charges to the item price. Confirm destination restrictions, return conditions and applicable import charges.

Clear answers before checkout

A few things
worth checking.

Use these answers alongside the current terms and information supplied by the seller and your purchasing service.

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product discovery list used to organize shopping links and comparison notes. A useful list may include categories, seller links, listed prices, measurements and QC references. A listing alone does not verify quality, authenticity or availability.

Is this an official Oopbuy website?

No. This is an independent CAFANS discovery guide. Its category links open CAFANS, and this page does not represent Oopbuy or the brands mentioned.

Does a spreadsheet price include shipping?

Do not assume that it does. Check the item price, domestic delivery, service charges, international shipping and any applicable taxes or duties separately before paying.

What should I check in QC photos?

Check the selected color and size, visible construction, stitching, labels, measurements and any obvious defects. QC photos show visible details but do not establish authenticity or guarantee durability.

Can every product link be ordered through Oopbuy?

Compatibility is not guaranteed. Confirm supported seller links, product restrictions, destination rules and current service availability with the purchasing service before ordering.
